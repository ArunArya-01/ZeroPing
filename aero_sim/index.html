AeroFlux

PHYSICS-GUIDED MACHINE LEARNING FOR AIRCRAFT FUEL CONSUMPTION PREDICTION

Predict aircraft fuel consumption from sparse flight data by combining aircraft-performance physics, dynamic mass representation, and machine learning.

Research Pipeline

01

Flight Data

Fused ADS-B and ACARS telemetry from EUROCONTROL PRC 2025 challenge. Sparse, noisy, incomplete.

↓
02

Physics Baseline

OpenAP first-principles fuel-flow estimate from aircraft type, speed, altitude, vertical rate, and reference mass.

↓
03

Dynamic Mass

Key R3 contribution: estimate aircraft mass in-flight from fuel-burn dynamics. Mass is rarely known but central to prediction.

↓
04

R3 Ensemble

Stacked gradient-boosted models (XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost) learn the residual between physics and reality.

↓
05

Distilled Model

Frozen teacher trains a compact MLP student. Runs in-browser via ONNX Runtime Web.

↓
06

Fuel Prediction

Per-interval fuel burn prediction validated against ground-truth labels.
